# Lumi Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 27, 2026**

Lumi `(@Lumi_a_bot)` is a AI assistant for telegram.

### Information Lumi Processes

When you interact with Lumi, the bot may process:

* Your Telegram user ID and display information provided by Telegram.
* Messages you send to Lumi.
* Messages and conversation context needed to generate AI responses.
* Commands, files, or other content you explicitly provide to Lumi.

### How Information Is Used

Lumi uses this information to:

* Generate responses to your messages.
* Maintain conversation context when enabled.
* Perform requested tasks such as web searches, file operations, or command execution.

### Third-Party Services

Depending on the configuration, Lumi may send message content or related information to third-party services used for AI processing, web search, or other requested functions.

These services may include AI model providers and search providers such as Tavily.

### Data Storage

Lumi does not intentionally sell or share personal information for advertising purposes.

Any conversation history, logs, or other data stored by Lumi are retained only as long as necessary for the bot's operation or configuration.

### Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information and access to Lumi. However, no system can guarantee complete security.

### Access and Deletion

Because Lumi is a private bot, access is restricted by its owner and configured group permissions.

For questions about data stored by Lumi or requests for deletion, contact the bot owner.

### Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Lumi's functionality or data handling changes.
